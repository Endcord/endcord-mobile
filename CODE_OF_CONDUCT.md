# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, the members, contributors and maintainers of Endcord, pledge to make taking part in our community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy
community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and respectful to other people, and to their time
- Being open to differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community as a whole

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind
- Trolling and deliberately derailing discussions
- Publishing other people's private information, such as an address or an account, without their explicit
  permission
- Sharing plugins, code or links meant to harm users, steal accounts or tokens, or harass people
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces: this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions),
the Endcord Discord server, and anywhere an individual officially represents the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour can be reported to the maintainers through the Endcord Discord server or
by opening a private report as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). All reports are reviewed promptly and
fairly, and the privacy and safety of the reporter are respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not
follow this Code of Conduct, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
